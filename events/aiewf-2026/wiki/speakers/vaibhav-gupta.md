# Vaibhav Gupta

**CEO &middot; Boundary**

Vaibhav Gupta is the co-founder at Boundary and building BAML, a new programming language that's agent first. Previously at D. E. Shaw, Google, and Microsoft. In his free time, Vaibhav dabbles in competitive table tennis and board games, and various aspects of compilers and VMs.
