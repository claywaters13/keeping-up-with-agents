# Zach Blumenfeld

**AI Research Engineer &middot; Neo4j**

Zach Blumenfeld is an AI/ML graph specialist at Neo4j who helps engineers, data scientists, and business leaders use graph technology for analytics and intelligent applications, including GraphRAG, agentic systems, fraud detection, entity resolution, and recommendation engines.
