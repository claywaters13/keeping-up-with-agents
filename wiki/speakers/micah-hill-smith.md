# Micah Hill-Smith

**CEO &middot; Artificial Analysis**

Co-Founder and CEO at Artificial Analysis, the leading independent AI benchmarking company. Artificial Analysis publishes benchmarks and analysis across agents, models, inference providers and hardware. Artificial Analysis maintains widely referenced leaderboards and evaluation frameworks that are regularly cited by frontier AI organizations, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, NVIDIA and others.
