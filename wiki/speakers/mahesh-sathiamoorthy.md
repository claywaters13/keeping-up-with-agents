# Mahesh Sathiamoorthy

**CEO &middot; Bespoke Labs**

Co-founder and CEO of Bespoke Labs, which is a data research lab. He created OpenThoughts, a widely used reasoning dataset, and Generative Retrieval, which is used across the industry to improve recommender systems. Previously at Google DeepMind.
